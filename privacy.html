The short version: Prayer App does not require an account and we do not operate a user-account or analytics database. Prayer times, the Tasbih counter, and the Fajr adhan notification are free to use with no sign-up. Your location, prayer logs, Tasbih counts, and settings stay on your device. If you subscribe to Premium, Google Play or the Apple App Store processes the purchase, and RevenueCat receives subscription information and an anonymous app user ID so the app can unlock and restore Premium. Your name and email are optional and are sent to RevenueCat only if you enter them yourself in Settings. Sentry receives limited crash diagnostics that are configured not to include your name, email, location, or advertising identifier. We show no ads, use no advertising or behavioral analytics trackers, and never sell personal data.

Retention and deletion in one line: We do not receive or retain data that stays only on your device. You can delete local data and request removal of optional RevenueCat contact details in the app at Settings → Account → “Delete account and data”. You can also request deletion by emailing technologiesrehman@gmail.com. Full details appear in Section 6, Data retention and Section 7, Data deletion.

RevenueCat, Inc. manages subscription status and entitlements across platforms. Prayer reminders and adhan notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use a third-party push-notification service to send these reminders.

7. Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time. Prayer App does not require an account, and you may use either of the following deletion methods.

Option A — delete data inside the app

  1. Open Prayer App.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Open the Account card.
  4. Tap “Delete account and data”.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

This immediately and permanently deletes the app data stored on your device, including settings, prayer logs, streaks, Tasbih counts, saved location, and the local profile. It also requests that RevenueCat clear any optional name and email you entered. If the RevenueCat request cannot be confirmed, the app reports a partial deletion and tells you to retry or contact us. Uninstalling the app also removes on-device app data, but does not by itself cancel a subscription or submit a deletion request to RevenueCat.

Option B — request deletion by email

  1. Email technologiesrehman@gmail.com.
  2. Use “Data deletion request” as the subject.
  3. If you entered an email in the app, send the request from that email address. Tell us whether you used Google Play or the Apple App Store. Do not send a password or full payment-card information.

We will acknowledge the request within seven days and complete an identifiable, verified deletion request within 30 days. We may ask for limited information needed to locate the RevenueCat customer record. If we cannot identify a record associated with you, we will explain that. A completed request deletes optional RevenueCat contact attributes and, where identifiable and not legally required to be retained, the associated RevenueCat customer record.

What deletion does not cover

  • Deletion does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play Subscriptions or the Apple App Store subscription settings to stop future billing.
  • Store and legally required transaction records may be retained. Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat, or TechnologiesRehman may retain limited transaction information for the period described in Section 6 when required for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.
  • Crash reports cannot normally be matched to you. They are configured without your name, email, location, or advertising identifier and expire under the Sentry retention period described in Section 6.
