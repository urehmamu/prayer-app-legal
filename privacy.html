Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Home Terms of Service

The short version: Prayer App has no servers and no required account. Prayer times, the Tasbih counter, and the Fajr adhan notification are free to use with no sign-up. Your location, prayer logs, tasbih counts, and settings never leave your device. If you choose to subscribe to Premium, Google Play or the Apple App Store handles the purchase and we receive your subscription status and an anonymous subscriber ID from RevenueCat. Adding your name and email is entirely optional and only happens if you enter them yourself in Settings — we send them to RevenueCat only when you do. We also receive anonymous crash reports (no name, email, or location) that help us fix bugs. We show no ads, use no analytics trackers, and never sell data. Ever.

1. Who we are

Prayer App (published on Google Play as prayer-app and on the Apple App Store as Salah: Prayer Times & Qibla) is developed and operated by TechnologiesRehman, based in Ontario, Canada (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question or request, contact technologiesrehman@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect and where it goes

Prayer times (using the app’s default calculation method), the Tasbih counter, and the Fajr adhan notification are free and available with no account, no sign-up, and no data entry. The rows below only apply if you choose to add optional details or subscribe to Premium.

DataPurposeWhere it is stored
Name & email (optional — only if you add them in Settings) Contacting you for support. Never required to use the app or to subscribe. Encrypted on your device; sent to RevenueCat as subscriber attributes only when you enter them
Subscription status, purchase history & anonymous app user ID Unlocking premium features, restoring purchases, fraud prevention RevenueCat (see §4); retained per §6
Location (GPS or manually chosen city) Calculating prayer times and Qibla direction Your device only — never transmitted to us or anyone else
Settings, prayer logs, streaks, tasbih counts App functionality Your device only
Motion & compass sensor readings Qibla compass Processed in real time on your device; never stored or transmitted
Anonymous crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS & app version) Finding and fixing bugs Sentry (see §4) — stripped of name, email, location, and identifiers

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contact lists, photos, or browsing data. The app contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking SDKs.

3. Payments

All payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other payment information. Their handling of your payment data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Our service providers

RevenueCat, Inc. manages subscriptions across platforms. RevenueCat receives your subscription status, an anonymous app user ID, and the name and email you entered at signup. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf and does not sell it. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) receives crash and error reports so we can find and fix bugs. These reports contain technical details about the error (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version). They are configured to contain no name, email, location, or advertising identifiers — we strip user-identifying fields before anything is sent. See the Sentry Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications

Prayer reminders and adhan notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No push-notification service receives your data.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data (settings, prayer logs, tasbih counts, your saved profile): you can delete it immediately in the app at Settings → Account → “Delete account and data”, or it is deleted permanently when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Optional contact details (name and email, if you added them): the in-app “Delete account and data” control clears these from RevenueCat. You can also email us at technologiesrehman@gmail.com and we will delete them within 30 days.
  • Purchase and subscription transaction records are kept by RevenueCat and the app stores for billing, tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes even after you delete your contact details or the app. Deleting your account does not delete these records and does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that directly in Google Play or the App Store.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Canada’s PIPEDA and California’s CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests are satisfied by uninstalling the app; for anything held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will action it. We do not and will not sell personal information.

8. Children

Prayer App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Your profile is stored using your device’s hardware-backed encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). Data sent to RevenueCat is encrypted in transit using TLS.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app’s release notes. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
