In short: you can delete everything yourself, inside the app, in about ten seconds. No account, no request form, and no waiting. If you would rather we did it, email technologiesrehman@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and we will complete it within 30 days.

Option A — delete it yourself in the app

This is the fastest route and it needs no account and no contact with us.

  1. Open Prayer App (Salah: Prayer Times & Qibla).
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Open the Account card.
  4. Tap “Delete account and data”.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Uninstalling the app also removes everything stored on your device.

Option B — ask us to delete it

  1. Email technologiesrehman@gmail.com, ideally from the email address you entered in the app.
  2. Use the subject line “Data deletion request”.
  3. Tell us you want your data deleted. You do not need to give a reason.

We confirm receipt within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. If we need more information to find your record we will ask; if no record exists for you, we will tell you that.

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Settings, prayer logs, streaks, tasbih counts, saved location and profile Deleted immediately and permanently. This data only ever existed on your device — we never received a copy of it.
Your optional name and email Deleted. Cleared from your device and from our subscription provider (RevenueCat). Immediate via Option A; within 30 days via Option B.
Subscription status and anonymous app user ID Kept for the life of the subscription and up to 24 months afterwards, so you can restore a purchase on a new device.
Purchase and subscription transaction records Kept for up to 7 years. Canadian tax, accounting, and consumer-protection law requires us to retain billing records, and they are also used for fraud prevention. These cannot be deleted on request while that obligation lasts.
Anonymous crash reports Deleted automatically after 90 days. They contain no name, email, location, or advertising identifier, so they cannot be linked back to you.

Two things to know before you delete

  • Deleting your data does not cancel your subscription. Cancel that separately in Google Play (Subscriptions) or the App Store, or you will continue to be billed.
  • Deleting does not remove the access you paid for. If you have an active subscription, you keep Premium after deleting your contact details.
